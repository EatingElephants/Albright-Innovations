How we work

One leak at a time, starting with the biggest.

  1. A conversation about what's eating your week

    About thirty minutes, on the phone or in person. You talk, we listen.

  2. We watch how work actually moves through the business

    From the first call to the final invoice. Where it waits, where it gets retyped, where it depends on someone remembering.

  3. We fix the biggest leak first

    One change, built around how you already work, with your team shown how to use it.

  4. We keep going while it's working

    Thirty to sixty days in, we know your business well, and the next fix is usually clear.

No long contracts. No forty-page reports.

How we figured this

We add the five sliders to get your hours a week, multiply by 52 for a year, then multiply by what you said an hour of your time is worth. The work-weeks line divides the yearly hours by 40. Nothing is weighted or inflated. It's your numbers, added up.

Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

What does an operations check involve?
We follow one job through your business, from the first call to the final invoice, and watch where it slows down or depends on someone remembering. Then you get a short list of what we'd fix first and why. Here's what we look at.
Do you replace our software?
Only if it's genuinely the problem. We start with what you have, and we don't make you switch tools that already work for you.
How long does it take?
The first conversation is about thirty minutes. The first fix usually lands within a few weeks. Bigger engagements run thirty to sixty days, with us learning your business as we go.
What does it cost?
It depends on scope. We figure it out together after the first conversation, and you get the price in writing before anything begins.
Where do you work?
Big Rapids and West Michigan, and Charlotte, Waxhaw and Union County in North Carolina. Much of this work happens remotely, so if you're somewhere else, tell us anyway.
