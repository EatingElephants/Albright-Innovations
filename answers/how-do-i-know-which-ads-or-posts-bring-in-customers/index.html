How do I know which ads or posts are bringing in customers?

Give each ad or post its own trail: a separate phone number, a tagged link, or a "how did you hear about us" that gets written down every single time. Then match each job back to the trail it came in on.

Last updated October 4, 2026

Three ways to leave a trail

MethodHow it worksEffort
Ask, every time"How did you hear about us?" on every call and form, written into the customer list. Not asked sometimes. Every time.None to set up, discipline to keep
Tracking phone numbersEach ad, the Facebook page and the website get their own number that forwards to your real one. The report shows which number rang.Some setup, plus a monthly fee that varies by provider
Tagged linksEach post or ad links to your site with a short tag on the end of the address. Your site's reports then show which post sent the visit and whether they filled out the form.Minutes per post once it's set up

Do the first one today. Add the other two when you're spending money on ads.

The step that's easy to skip

Knowing where the call came from isn't enough. You need to know where the job came from. That means the source has to travel with the customer from the first call through the quote to the invoice. When it does, you can total up invoices by source at the end of the month and see, in dollars, what each ad or post was worth.

What a useful report shows

  • Calls, messages and forms this month, by source
  • How many of each became a quote, and how many became a job
  • Revenue by source
  • What you spent on each source, so you can see the return

Four lines.

Common mistakes

  • Counting likes and reach instead of calls and jobs
  • Asking "how did you hear about us" on the form but not on the phone
  • Running three ads to the same phone number, so there's no way to tell them apart
  • Judging an ad after a week. In our experience, most ads need about a month before you can see a pattern.

Setting this up is the lead tracking and reporting piece of what we do. We work out with you how the source can follow each customer from the first call to the invoice, using the tools you already have where we can. We keep the report to four lines.
