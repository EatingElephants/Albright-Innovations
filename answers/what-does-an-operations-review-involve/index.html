What does an operations review involve?

A walk-through of how work moves through your business, from the first call to the final invoice. The first conversation takes about 30 minutes, and you get a one-page list of what we'd fix first.

Last updated October 4, 2026

What we look at

We follow one job through your business and watch where it slows down or depends on someone remembering.

  1. How leads come in. Phone, Facebook, forms, referrals. Who answers, how fast, and what happens when nobody can.
  2. Where customer information lives. Phone contacts, a notebook, the invoicing app, your head. How many places the same name gets typed.
  3. Quotes. How long they take, how they're sent, and what happens if the customer doesn't answer.
  4. Scheduling. How a job gets on the calendar and how the crew finds out.
  5. Invoicing and getting paid. When invoices go out, how, and how long you wait.
  6. Follow-up. Review requests, reminders for repeat work, and reaching past customers.
  7. The tools you already pay for. Often the fix is connecting what you have, not buying something new.

What we need from you

  • Thirty minutes, by phone, video or at the shop
  • A rough idea of calls a week, jobs a month and your average job value. Guesses are fine.
  • The names of the tools you use now: phone, calendar, quoting, invoicing, customer list

What you get

A one-page list, in plain words, of what we'd fix first and why, ordered by what's costing you the most time or the most jobs. It's yours whether or not you hire us. Our 2-minute checkup gives you a first version of that list right now.

What happens next, if you want it to

We scope the work together, agree on a price before anything starts, and then build it: the tools picked and connected, the workflows written down, your team shown how to use them. Then we keep it running and show you the results as they come in. Here's how a project runs from start to finish.
