Answers

Do you work with businesses anywhere in the country?

Yes. Based in Big Rapids, Michigan and the Charlotte, North Carolina area, working with businesses anywhere in the U.S. Most of the work happens online, so where you are matters less than what you need.

Last updated October 14, 2026

What's the same wherever you are

Almost everything. Social media, ads, websites and the 24/7 assistant are built and run online no matter where you are. Setting up and connecting your phones, customer list, quoting and invoicing is done remotely too; we share a screen and work through it together. The operations check happens over a video call, following one job from first call to final invoice the same way we would in person.

What's different

  • Photos for your posts and website come from you or a photographer near you. We'll tell you exactly what to shoot.
  • Training your team happens over video, in short sessions.
  • We keep normal Eastern time hours. If you're a few time zones away, we'll find an overlap that works.

Who it's a good fit for

Small and medium businesses that are already busy and want the office side to need less of them: trades, auto, home services, shops with a counter and a phone. Where you are matters less than what you need. Tell us both and we'll give you an honest answer.
