That's more than most work weeks. Worth a second look at the numbers.

Most estimates put admin at 15 to 20 hours a week (Xero). One 2023 survey of 251 owners found 36% of the week, about 16 hours (Time etc). Not every missed caller is lost, so treat the revenue figure as a ceiling.

How we figured this

Your week: we add the five hour sliders, multiply by 52 for a year, then by what an hour of your time is worth. Nothing is weighted or inflated.

Your phone: calls a week times the share you miss, times 52, divided by 12, gives missed calls a month. Missed calls times the share of answered calls that become work, times your average job value, gives the revenue at risk. It assumes a missed caller was as likely to hire you as an answered one and doesn't count the ones who call back, so it's a ceiling, not a forecast.
