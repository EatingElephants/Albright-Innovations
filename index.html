About $9,360 a month riding on calls nobody picked up.

Not every missed caller is lost, so treat this as the ceiling. The research shows how many don't come back.

How we figured this

Calls a week times the share you miss gives missed calls a week. We multiply by 52 for a year and divide by 12 for a month. Missed calls times the share of answered calls that become work gives jobs at risk, and jobs times your average job value gives the revenue. It assumes a missed caller was as likely to hire you as an answered one, and it doesn't count the ones who try again, so it's a ceiling, not a forecast.
