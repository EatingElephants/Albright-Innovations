About

Twelve years building what a growing business needs. Now I build it for yours.

I'm Joshua Albright. I started Albright Innovations with a simple idea: help a handful of good businesses reach more customers and run more efficiently, so everybody wins.

I earned my marketing degree at UNC Charlotte and worked in the restaurant business through the 2008 financial crisis. Then I joined a four-month-old startup as a sales rep and spent the next twelve years helping it grow. There wasn't much I didn't touch. I rebuilt the company's marketing. I created its contracts department from the ground up and ran it. As Director of Operations, I built the full customer workflow, from the first sales call to the close and every follow-up for the life of the contract. I led the move of about a million dollars of inventory into a new 8,000-square-foot warehouse, built the returns department from scratch, modernized how the warehouse ran, and worked alongside accounting so they understood the operations side of the business.

The part I'm proudest of is the part I'm doing again now. I sit down with an owner, listen to where they want to take the business, and then actually build it: the workflows, the follow-up, the posts, the website.

What I enjoy most is the relief. A business owner who's been carrying too much, and a few weeks later isn't. That's why I do this.

Along the way, a lot of good people helped me. They taught me what good business looks like: fair, honest, and a win for both sides. That's how I run Albright Innovations.
