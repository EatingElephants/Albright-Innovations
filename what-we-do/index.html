What we do

Two sides of your business. We help with both.

Getting found, and running smoothly once people find you. Here's what each service is, what you get, and who it fits. Every business is different, so the details always come from a conversation with you first.

Always Improving The office side Authentic Influence Being found

Always Improving

The office side of the business. If the work gets done but too much of it depends on you remembering, start here.

  • Operations check

    What it is
    We follow one job through your business, from the first call to the final invoice, and look for where it slows down or depends on someone's memory.
    What you get
    A one-page list of what we'd fix first and why, in plain words, ordered by what's costing you the most. It's yours to keep whether or not we work together. What we look at.
    A good fit if
    You're busy, the work is good, and the office side feels held together with sticky notes.

  • A 24/7 assistant for your website and phone

    What it is
    An automated assistant on your website that answers common questions. It takes the customer's name, number and what they need, and sends it to you right away, day or night. It can also pick up your phone line when a call goes unanswered.
    What you get
    Set up for your business: your services, your area, how you work. You approve what it says before it goes live, and it tells people it's automated. Leads arrive by email, customers can send photos, and it's month to month.
    A good fit if
    Calls and messages come in while you're on a job or after hours, and some of them never get a reply.

  • The right tools for how you work

    What it is
    Help choosing, setting up and cleaning up the tools behind the business: your customer list, scheduling, phones, quoting, invoicing and job tracking.
    What you get
    Tools that fit how you already work. The ones you have stay wherever they do the job, and your team is shown how to use what's there.
    A good fit if
    You're paying for software nobody uses, or the business runs on a spreadsheet and your text messages.

  • Workflows and follow-up

    What it is
    Writing down how work moves through your business, from the first call to the close to the follow-up, so it doesn't live only in your head.
    What you get
    Clear steps your team can follow, a plan for following up after quotes and jobs, and fewer questions that only you can answer.
    A good fit if
    Nothing moves when you're off the clock, or follow-up happens only when someone remembers.

  • Lead tracking and reporting

    What it is
    Working out where your customers come from, so you know which ads, posts and referrals bring in paying work.
    What you get
    A short, regular report with the few numbers you care about, built from the tools you already use wherever we can. How it works.
    A good fit if
    You're spending money on ads and can't say which ones pay for themselves.

Authentic Influence

Being found by the people around you. If customers don't see you online, or what they see looks out of date, start here.

  • Social media management

    What it is
    We plan your posts, write them in your voice, schedule them and answer comments.
    What you get
    A page that stays active and looks like the busy business you are, built from your real work and your real people, without you living on it.
    A good fit if
    Your page hasn't had a new post in months, or posting is the job that always slides. Is it worth paying someone?

  • Google Business Profile and reviews

    What it is
    Your listing on Google, kept complete and current, with regular posts and a steady way of asking happy customers for reviews.
    What you get
    Showing up when people nearby search for what you do, and replies to reviews in your voice.
    A good fit if
    People search for your trade near you and someone else's listing comes up first.

  • Social ads

    What it is
    Facebook and Instagram ads aimed at people near you who are likely to need what you offer.
    What you get
    Ads set up and watched for you, with plain reporting on what they bring in. We don't guarantee numbers; we show you the results as they come in.
    A good fit if
    You've tried boosting posts and couldn't tell whether it worked.

  • Websites

    What it is
    A new site, or a refresh of the one you have, that says who you are, what you do and how to reach you.
    What you get
    A clean, current site that works on a phone, kept up to date after it launches. If you'd like, we host it for you with the assistant built in.
    A good fit if
    Your site hasn't changed in years, or you don't have one yet. What a small business site needs.

Both sides, together

You can start with one side and add the other later. When we look after both, one team sees the whole business, from the first post a customer sees to the final invoice. The marketing brings in work the office is ready to handle.

What we don't do

  • We don't promise numbers. We show you the results as they come in.
  • We don't make you switch tools that already work for you.
  • We don't take on more clients than we can look after well.
