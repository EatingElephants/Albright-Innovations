Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Joshua Albright, doing business as Albright Innovations ("Albright Innovations," "we," "us," "our"), collects, uses and shares information. It applies to our website at https://albright-innovations.com, our landing pages, our forms, our done-for-you services and our 24/7 assistant plans. We are based in Big Rapids, Michigan and the Charlotte, North Carolina area. You can contact us at joshua@albright-innovations.com.

2. Two kinds of people this covers

People who visit our website, fill out our forms, contact us or buy from us. For this information, we decide how it is used.

People who chat with an assistant that one of our customers has set up on their own website or social pages. Our customer decides what the assistant collects and why. We and our service providers handle that information on the customer’s behalf. If you used an assistant on another business’s website, please read that business’s privacy notice, and contact that business first for any request about your information.

3. Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Contact and business details: your name, business name, email address, phone number, business type, area, and your website or Google or Facebook page link.
  • Messages you send us and anything else you choose to tell us, including by form, email or chat.
  • Account and billing details. Payments are handled by our payment processor (Stripe). We do not store your full card number.
  • Content you give us to build your website or set up your assistant, such as text, photos, logos and documents.

Information collected automatically:

  • Device and usage information such as your browser, device type, IP address, pages viewed, time on page and referring site.
  • Ad and campaign information, such as which ad or link brought you to us (for example, UTM tags and ad-click identifiers).
  • Cookies, pixels and similar technologies (see Section 6).

Information from chats with an assistant (for our customers’ visitors):

  • The messages typed into the chat, and the name, phone number, email address or other details the visitor chooses to share.
  • Basic technical information such as the date, time and page where the chat took place.
  • For assistants connected to Facebook or Instagram, the messages and basic profile details Meta passes along to the page owner.

Please do not share sensitive information such as payment card numbers, health information, government ID numbers or passwords in a chat or form.

4. How we use information

  • Provide, set up, run and support our website, services and assistant plans.
  • Respond to your questions and requests, and follow up on forms you submit.
  • Process payments, send receipts and manage your account and trial.
  • Send service messages such as confirmations, billing notices, outage notices and check-ins about your account.
  • Send you marketing about our services, where the law allows. You can opt out at any time (see Section 11).
  • Measure and improve our website, ads and services, and understand which ads bring in leads.
  • Keep our services secure, prevent fraud and misuse, and follow the law.
  • For assistants: deliver chat replies and send leads and alerts to the business that set up the assistant.

5. Legal bases and consent

Where the law asks for a legal basis, we rely on our contract with you, our legitimate interest in running and improving our business, your consent where required (for example, for certain cookies or marketing), and legal obligations.

6. Cookies, pixels and analytics

We use cookies and similar tools to make our site work, measure traffic and measure our advertising. These include the Meta Pixel, which tells Meta when someone who clicked our Facebook or Instagram ad takes an action on our site, such as submitting a form. Meta may use that information to show you ads and to measure results, under Meta’s own policies. We may also use analytics tools such as Google Analytics.

You can control cookies in your browser settings, and you can manage ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram settings and through the tools provided by the Digital Advertising Alliance (https://optout.aboutads.info). Blocking some cookies may affect how our site works. We respond to opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control where the law requires it.

7. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run our business: the assistant platform (our assistant platform provider), payment processing (Stripe), Google (for email, documents, forms and analytics), Meta (Facebook, Instagram and the Pixel), and email, form, scheduling and website hosting tools.
  • Language model providers that generate the assistant’s replies. Chat messages are sent to them through the assistant platform so a reply can be produced.
  • For assistant chats: the business that set up the assistant.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers, accountants and insurers, when needed.
  • Authorities or other parties when the law requires it, or to protect rights, safety and security.
  • A buyer or successor if our business is sold or reorganized.

These providers may use the information only to provide services to us and under their own privacy terms.

8. How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as we need it for the reasons above, including to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. Typically that means for as long as you are a customer, plus a reasonable period afterward. Lead and inquiry records that do not turn into customers are kept for a reasonable period and then deleted or anonymized. Chat records for an assistant are kept by the business that runs it for as long as it chooses, and by the assistant platform under its own retention practices.

9. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, such as access controls and trusted providers, to protect information. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

10. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy.
  • Correct information that is wrong.
  • Delete your information, subject to legal and business record-keeping needs.
  • Stop using your information for marketing.

To make a request, email joshua@albright-innovations.com. We may need to confirm who you are first, and we will respond within 45 days (or sooner if the law requires). Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under your state’s privacy law, including the right to appeal our decision. We will not treat you differently for using your rights. If your request is about an assistant chat on another business’s site, we may refer you to that business.

11. Marketing messages

If you give us your email address or phone number, we may contact you about your inquiry and our services. Each marketing email has an unsubscribe link. You can also reply STOP to a text message or email us to opt out. We will still send you messages about your account and billing.

12. Children

Our website and services are meant for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, please email us and we will delete it.

13. Where information is stored

We and our providers are based mainly in the United States, and your information may be processed and stored here. If you use our services from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to the United States.

14. Links to other sites

Our website may link to other websites, such as Facebook, Google and Stripe. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new "last updated" date. If a change is significant, we will tell you by email or on our website.

16. Contact us

Questions or requests about privacy? Email joshua@albright-innovations.com.
