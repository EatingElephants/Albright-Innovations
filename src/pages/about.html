About

I've built this before. Now I build it for businesses like yours.

I'm Joshua Albright, and I run Albright Innovations. I help a handful of good businesses get more customers and run more smoothly, and I do the work myself.

Where I come from

I grew up around entrepreneurs. My family runs a rental business in Big Rapids, Michigan, now in its third generation, and my mother ran a business of her own. That's where I learned what a small business actually runs on: the phone ringing during dinner, quotes written at the kitchen table, and the owner being the only one who knows where everything is. It's also where I learned that a fair deal is one that works for both sides.

What I did for twelve years

I earned my marketing degree at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte in 2006. A few years later I started in sales at a startup and stayed twelve years, moving from sales to marketing to contracts to Director of Operations while the company grew to over $10 million a year.

There wasn't much I didn't touch. I rebuilt the company's marketing. I created its contracts department from the ground up and ran it. As Director of Operations I built the full customer workflow, from the first sales call through the close and every follow-up for the life of the contract, and I worked alongside accounting so the money side and the operations side understood each other.

The part I'm proudest of is the part I'm doing again now: sitting down with the people doing the work, listening to where they want to go, and then building the systems that get them there.

Why I started this

Because the thing I enjoy most is the relief. A business owner who has been carrying too much, and a few weeks later isn't. The calls get answered, the quotes and invoices go out on time, and the Facebook page looks like the busy business it is. That's why I do this.

How I work

  • I tell you what I'd do with your business and why, before you decide anything.
  • I don't guarantee numbers. I show you results as they come in.
  • I keep my client list small on purpose, so every client gets my attention.
  • I use plain words. If I can't explain it simply, I don't understand it well enough yet.
