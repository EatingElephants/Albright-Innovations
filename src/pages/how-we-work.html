How we work

You see our thinking before you decide anything.

Every project starts the same way: we look at how work moves through your business, tell you what we'd fix first, and put a price on it in writing. Then we build it and keep it running.

  1. Tell us what's going on

    Through the panel on this site, by email, or on a 30-minute call. A sentence or two about what's eating up your week is plenty to start. If you've used the calculators or the checkup, those numbers come along.

  2. The operations check

    We follow one job through your business, from the first call to the final invoice, and watch where it slows down or depends on someone remembering. Thirty minutes, no charge. Here's what we look at.

  3. A one-page list

    What we'd fix first and why, in plain words, ordered by what's costing you the most time or the most jobs. It's yours to keep whether or not we work together.

  4. A price in writing

    We pick a starting point together, usually the top one or two items on the list, and you get the scope, the price and the timeline before anything begins. How we think about cost.

  5. The build

    The fixes we agreed on, built around how you already work, using the tools you already have where they fit. Workflows written down. Follow-up that doesn't depend on memory. Your team shown how to use it.

  6. Being seen

    If marketing is part of the plan: the posts planned and scheduled, the ads set up and watched, the website rebuilt or brought current. Everything in your voice, with your real work.

  7. Keeping it running

    We check in on a schedule, fix what drifts, and show you the numbers as they come in: calls answered, quotes sent, jobs by source. No guarantees, just the results.

What we need from you

  • Thirty minutes for the first conversation
  • Rough numbers: calls a week, jobs a month, average job value. Guesses are fine.
  • The names of the tools you use now
  • Honest answers about what's working and what isn't

What you can expect from us

  • Plain words. No jargon, no guarantees we can't keep.
  • Our honest take, including "you don't need this" when that's true
  • A small client list, so you get our attention
  • A fair deal that works for both sides

Two ways to start

The operations check if the office side is what's wearing you down. A website preview if being found is the problem: we mock up a new homepage for your business so you can see what it could look like, free and with no obligation. Either way, you see our thinking first.
