In both areas we're happy to meet at your shop, walk the floor and see how the work actually moves.

What's the same for a remote client

Almost everything. Social media, ads and websites are built and run online no matter where you are. Setting up and connecting your phones, customer list, quoting and invoicing is done remotely too; we share a screen and work through it together. The operations review happens over a video call, following one job from first call to final invoice the same way we would in person.

What's different

  • Photos for your posts and website come from you or a local photographer we help you find. We'll tell you exactly what to shoot.
  • Training your team happens over video, in short sessions, rather than in one afternoon at the shop.
  • We keep normal Eastern time hours. If you're three time zones away, we'll find an overlap that works.

Who it's a good fit for

Small and medium businesses that are already busy and want the office side to run without them: trades, auto, home services, shops with a counter and a phone. If that sounds like you, where you are matters less than what you need. Tell us both and we'll give you an honest answer.
