The other reason: it isn't happening

For a lot of owners the honest answer isn't "I spend three hours a week on it." It's "I meant to, and the last post was in March." In that case the question isn't whether to pay someone. It's whether you want the page working for you at all. A page that posts steadily brings in calls. A page that doesn't costs you the ones who looked and moved on.

When it's not worth it

  • You enjoy it, you're good at it, and you'd do it anyway
  • Your customers don't look for you on Facebook or Instagram, and you've checked that rather than assumed it
  • The rest of the business can't handle more calls right now

What to expect from whoever does it

  • Posts that sound like your business, built from your real jobs, not stock photos
  • A plan you can see ahead of time, so nothing surprises you
  • Comments and messages answered, not just posts published
  • A short monthly note on what got attention and what brought in calls
