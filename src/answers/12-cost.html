How we figure it out together

  1. You tell us what's going on. Through the panel on this site, by email, or on a 30-minute call.
  2. We do the operations check. We follow a job through your business and come back with a one-page list of what we'd fix first. No charge.
  3. We pick a starting point. Usually the one or two items on that list that are costing you the most. Not everything at once.
  4. You get a price in writing. What's included, what it costs, and how long it takes, before anything starts.

What affects the price

  • How much is being set up. Connecting a phone system to a customer list is a smaller job than building the whole first-call-to-invoice workflow.
  • Whether it's a project or ongoing. A website rebuild is a project. Running your social media or watching your ads every month is ongoing.
  • How many people need training. A two-person shop and a twelve-person crew are different afternoons.
  • What you already have. If the tools you pay for now can do the job, we connect them instead of replacing them.

What you'll always know up front

  • The price, before work starts, in writing
  • What's included and what isn't
  • What we'd do first and what can wait
  • How we'll show you results as they come in

We don't guarantee numbers for your business and we don't do pressure. We keep our client list small on purpose, and every deal has to be a fair one for both sides. If a project isn't worth it for you, we'll say so.
