Do the first one today. Add the other two when you're spending money on ads.

The step most businesses skip

Knowing where the call came from isn't enough. You need to know where the job came from. That means the source has to travel with the customer from the first call through the quote to the invoice. When it does, you can total up invoices by source at the end of the month and see, in dollars, what each ad or post was worth.

What a useful dashboard shows

  • Calls, messages and forms this month, by source
  • How many of each became a quote, and how many became a job
  • Revenue by source
  • What you spent on each source, so you can see the return

Four lines. If a report has forty, nobody reads it.

Mistakes we see a lot

  • Counting likes and reach instead of calls and jobs
  • Asking "how did you hear about us" on the form but not on the phone, where most leads come in
  • Running three ads to the same phone number, so there's no way to tell them apart
  • Judging an ad after a week. Most need a month to show a pattern.
