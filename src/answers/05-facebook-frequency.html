Why we start at once a week

It's a pace most owners can keep up. We'd rather see a page post every week for a year than every day for a month.

What to post

Real work, in plain words.

  • A job you finished this week, with a photo and two sentences about it
  • Before and after, from the same angle
  • A person on your team and what they do
  • One useful tip a customer asked you about recently
  • A review someone left, with a thank-you
  • Hours, holidays, weather closures and other things people actually need to know

What matters more than frequency

  • Answer comments and messages. Most consumers want brands to respond on social media within 24 hours or sooner (Sprout Social).
  • Keep the basics current. Hours, phone number, address and services on the page itself.
  • Sound like yourself. Post your real work and your real people.

If the posting isn't happening at all, that's the problem to solve first. Our answer on paying someone to manage it walks through when that makes sense.
