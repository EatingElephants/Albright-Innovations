The six things, explained

  1. What you do. In the first sentence, in plain words. "Residential plumbing in Big Rapids" beats "Solutions for all your plumbing needs."
  2. Where you do it. The towns and counties you serve, written out. People search by town, and so do the answer tools people use now.
  3. Proof you're real. Photos of your work and your people, your name, your reviews. Stock photos signal the opposite.
  4. A way to reach you on every page. Phone number at the top, a short form, and both tappable on a phone.
  5. Current hours and prices where they apply. If something changes with the season, the site should change with it.
  6. A site that works on a phone. Most of your visitors are on one. Big text, big buttons, fast loading.

What you can skip

  • A blog you won't keep up. An empty blog with two posts from 2021 hurts more than no blog.
  • A slideshow at the top. People scroll past them.
  • Pages for every tiny variation of a service. Fewer, clearer pages win.
  • Pop-ups asking for an email before someone has read a word.
